# Compile The Tradeoff Ledger

Read the three goal proposals and their scorecards. Publish a normalized
tradeoff ledger that puts every goal on the same scale:

- One row per goal per dimension, with the scorecard's score and a
  one-line justification.
- Disagreements between a proposal's self-assessment and its scorecard,
  named explicitly.
- Each goal's single biggest unverified assumption, carried forward from
  its scorecard.

Do not select a goal. The ledger is evidence for arbitration, not a
verdict. Include the exact lowercase `author:` byline near the top of the
artifact.
