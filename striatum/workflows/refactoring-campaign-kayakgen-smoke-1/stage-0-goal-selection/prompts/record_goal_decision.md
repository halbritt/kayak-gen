# Record The Goal Decision

Publish the campaign's goal decision artifact. This is the input contract
for stage 1 (the plan gate): the plan holder reads this artifact and
nothing else from this run as authoritative.

Include:

- The selected goal, restated as one named, behavior-preserving structural
  change with its bounded surface.
- Provenance: which proposal won, the arbitration rationale in two
  sentences, and the dissent verdict.
- The frozen surfaces the goal must not cross, copied forward from the
  problem brief and narrowed to the goal's blast radius.
- The verification commands stage 1 must baseline against.
- Explicit non-goals: the runner-up goals and anything the dissent review
  carved out.

Follow the decision artifact's V1 front-matter schema; the publisher
refuses invalid front matter. Include the exact lowercase `author:` byline
near the top of the artifact.
