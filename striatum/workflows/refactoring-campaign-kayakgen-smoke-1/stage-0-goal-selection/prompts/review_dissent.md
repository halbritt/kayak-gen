# Review Dissent

Try to falsify the arbitration before the goal decision is recorded. Read
the arbitrator synthesis, the tradeoff ledger, and the proposals, then
publish a dissent review.

Attack the strongest available line:

- Is the selected goal actually behavior-preserving, or does it smuggle a
  feature, bug fix, schema change, or dependency upgrade under "refactor"?
- Is the preservation-verifiability claim real — do the named tests and
  verification commands exist in the current tree?
- Did the arbitration ignore a frozen-surface risk or a cheaper goal with
  comparable payoff?
- Is the goal too broad to execute as bounded slices?

Render a single verdict: `accept`, `accept_with_findings`, or
`needs_revision` with the specific defect arbitration must address. Do not
relitigate dimensions where you merely disagree with a defensible score.
Include the exact lowercase `author:` byline near the top of the artifact.
